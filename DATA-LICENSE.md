## SPOT imagery: Etalab Open Licence 2.0

The frames in `data/frames/swh-spot1-4-l1a-panchromatic/` and
`data/frames/swh-spot5-l1a-panchromatic/` are cut, aligned and
contrast-stretched from SPOT 1–5 Level 1A scenes of the Spot World Heritage
archive, distributed by CNES through DINAMIS under the
[Etalab Open Licence 2.0](https://www.data.gouv.fr/pages/legal/licences/etalab-2.0).

Credit: _SPOT 1–4 © CNES_ or _SPOT 5 © CNES_, with the year of the image.

[Copernicus Sentinel data terms](https://sentinels.copernicus.eu/documents/247904/690755/Sentinel_Data_Legal_Notice).
